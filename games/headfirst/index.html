Headfirst

Predators are hunting you. Take them down for coins, then spend them on weapons and upgrades below. Each run you carry one gun or one melee weapon, plus bombs: right-click or press E on desktop, or tap the 💣 button on a phone. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, hold the mouse button to aim, then let go to fire a burst, or just tap to auto-aim at the nearest animal. Your three shots recharge over time. On a phone, left thumb moves, and you drag the right thumb to aim and let go to fire. Stronger animals take more hits.
